# Returns

## Medicines cannot be returned

By law, dispensed medicines **cannot be returned or exchanged** once they
leave our pharmacy, even unopened, except in the specific cases below. This
is a regulatory requirement, not a Nimbus Health policy choice, and applies
regardless of packaging condition.

## When a return is allowed

- The wrong medicine, wrong strength, or wrong quantity was dispatched (a
  dispensing error on our side).
- The item arrived damaged, or a cold-chain medicine arrived visibly
  compromised (packaging opened, cold packs warm).
- The order was cancelled before dispatch (see Cancellation).

In these cases, contact support within **48 hours** with photos, and we
arrange collection and a full refund or replacement at no cost to you.

## Non-medicine items

Over-the-counter items that are not medicines — supplements sold without a
prescription requirement, health devices, personal care — follow a standard
14-day return policy provided they are unopened and in original packaging.
