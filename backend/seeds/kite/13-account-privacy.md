# Account and Privacy

## Your account

Create an account to track orders, save addresses, and manage Kite Club
points. Guest checkout is available, but guest orders cannot be tracked in an
account area and returns must be started through the emailed order link.

## Changing your details

Email address, phone number, and saved addresses can be changed in Account
settings. A delivery address on an order that has already been dispatched
cannot be changed — contact the courier directly using your tracking number.

## Data we hold

We store your name, contact details, delivery addresses, order history, and
Kite Club balance. We do not store card numbers.

## Marketing

Marketing emails and SMS are opt-in. Unsubscribe from any email footer or by
replying STOP to an SMS. Transactional messages about an active order are sent
regardless of marketing preference.

## Deleting your account

Request deletion from Account settings or by contacting support. We remove
personal data within 30 days, except order records we are required to retain
for tax purposes. Deleting an account forfeits any unredeemed Kite Club
points.
