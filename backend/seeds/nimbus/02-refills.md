# Refills and Repeat Orders

## Setting up a refill

For ongoing medication, set a refill reminder from your order page. We text
and email **3 days before** your current supply is due to run out.

## Auto-refill

Auto-refill is available for most non-controlled medicines and automatically
places and charges an order on your reminder date, using your saved payment
method and address. You can pause or cancel auto-refill at any time from
account settings — changes take effect from the next scheduled cycle, not
retroactively.

Schedule H1 and controlled medicines are **never** eligible for auto-refill;
each order needs a fresh pharmacist review (see Prescriptions).

## Refill limits

A single prescription authorises a fixed number of refills, set by your
doctor and printed on the prescription. Once that count is used, a new
prescription is required — we track this automatically and will tell you if
an order can't proceed because your refills are exhausted.

## Early refills

We can process a refill up to **5 days before** your supply is due to run out,
to allow for delivery time. Earlier than that requires your pharmacist's
approval, typically because insurance or regulation limits how early a
controlled substance can be redispensed.
