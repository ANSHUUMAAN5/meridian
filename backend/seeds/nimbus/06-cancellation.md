# Order Cancellation

## Before pharmacist review

An order can be cancelled free of charge any time before a pharmacist begins
reviewing it — usually within the first hour of placing it. Use the Cancel
order button on your order page.

## During or after pharmacist review

Once a pharmacist has reviewed and approved a prescription order, it is
prepared for dispatch and can no longer be cancelled through self-service,
because controlled and prescription stock has been allocated and, in some
cases, opened for dispensing. Contact support immediately if there's a
genuine problem — a pharmacist will assess whether cancellation is still
possible on a case-by-case basis.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

A successful cancellation is refunded in full to the original payment method
within 5-7 business days.

## Auto-refill cancellation

Cancelling a single auto-refill cycle does not cancel the underlying
auto-refill subscription — turn that off separately in account settings if
you don't want future cycles to continue.
