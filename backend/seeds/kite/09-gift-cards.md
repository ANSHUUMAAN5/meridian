# Gift Cards

## Purchase and delivery

Gift cards are available in denominations of ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,500, and ₹5,000.
They are delivered by email, usually within minutes, to the address given at
checkout.

## Validity

Gift cards are valid for **12 months** from the date of purchase. The expiry
date is printed on the card and shown in your account. Expired gift cards
cannot be extended or reinstated.

## Using a gift card

Enter the code at checkout. The balance is applied to the order total
including shipping. If the order exceeds the balance, pay the difference by
any accepted method. Multiple gift cards can be combined on one order.

Any remaining balance stays on the card for future orders.

## Restrictions

- Gift cards are **not refundable** and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- If an order paid for with a gift card is returned, the refund is issued as
  store credit to the same gift card, not to a bank account.
- Lost or stolen gift cards can be reissued only if you have the original
  order number.
