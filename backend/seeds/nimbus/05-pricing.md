# Pricing and Generic Substitution

## Generic substitution

Where permitted and where a doctor's prescription does not explicitly
prohibit it, we may dispense a generic equivalent of a branded medicine at a
lower price, containing the same active ingredient and strength. This is
noted clearly on your order and invoice. If your prescription says "no
substitution" or your doctor has specified a particular brand, we dispense
exactly what was prescribed.

## Insurance and reimbursement

We do not bill insurance directly. We provide a GST-compliant invoice with
every order, formatted for reimbursement claims with most Indian insurers.
Check with your insurer on what documentation they require.

## Price matching

We do not price-match other pharmacies. Prices update periodically based on
supplier cost and are shown at checkout before payment.

## Discounts

First-order discounts and periodic promotions are shown at checkout when
eligible. Discounts do not apply to Schedule H1 or controlled medicines,
in line with regulations on their sale.
