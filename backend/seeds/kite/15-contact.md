# Contacting Support

## Hours

Our support team is available **Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm IST**. We are
closed on Sundays and national public holidays.

## Channels

| Channel | Best for | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat | Quick questions during opening hours | Usually under 5 minutes |
| Email (help@kiteandco.in) | Anything needing photos or documents | Within 24 hours on business days |
| Phone (1800-000-4471) | Urgent delivery problems | Immediate during opening hours |

## Before you contact us

Have your **order number** ready — it begins with KC and appears in your
confirmation email. Most questions about delivery timing are answered by the
tracking link.

## Escalation

If you are unhappy with how an issue was handled, reply to your existing
support email and ask for it to be escalated. A senior member of the team
reviews escalations within 2 business days.

## What we cannot help with

We cannot change a delivery address after dispatch, reroute a parcel already
with the courier, or expedite an order that has already left the warehouse.
Those requests must go to the courier directly.
