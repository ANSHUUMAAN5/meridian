# Out of Stock and Pre-orders

## Out of stock

Items that sell out show as unavailable and cannot be added to the cart. Use
**Notify me** on the product page to be emailed if it is restocked. This is
not a reservation — stock is allocated on a first-come basis when it arrives.

We restock core styles roughly every 4-6 weeks. Seasonal and limited styles
are usually not restocked.

## Pre-orders

Some items can be pre-ordered before stock arrives. The expected dispatch date
is shown on the product page and in your order confirmation.

- Pre-orders are charged in full at the time of ordering.
- Pre-orders can be cancelled at any time before dispatch for a full refund.
- COD is not available on pre-orders.
- If a pre-order is delayed by more than 14 days beyond the stated date, we
  email you and you may cancel for a full refund.

## Mixed orders

If an order contains both in-stock and pre-order items, the whole order ships
together on the pre-order date. To receive the in-stock items sooner, place
them as a separate order.
