# Sizing Guide

Kite & Co uses standard Indian sizing. Measurements below are body
measurements, not garment measurements — the garment includes ease.

## Women's tops and dresses

| Size | Bust (in) | Waist (in) | Hip (in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| XS | 32 | 25 | 35 |
| S | 34 | 27 | 37 |
| M | 36 | 29 | 39 |
| L | 38 | 31 | 41 |
| XL | 40 | 33 | 43 |
| XXL | 42 | 35 | 45 |

## Men's shirts

| Size | Chest (in) | Neck (in) |
|---|---|---|
| S | 38 | 15 |
| M | 40 | 15.5 |
| L | 42 | 16 |
| XL | 44 | 16.5 |
| XXL | 46 | 17 |

## Between sizes

Our knitwear runs slightly fitted; size up if you are between sizes or prefer
a relaxed fit. Woven shirts and denim run true to size.

## Fit issues

If an item does not fit, use the free exchange to swap sizes. See Exchanges.
Each product page lists the model's height and the size they are wearing.
