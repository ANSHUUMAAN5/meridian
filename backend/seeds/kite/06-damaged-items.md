# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

## Report within 48 hours

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or containing the wrong item, report
it **within 48 hours of delivery**. Reports after that window are handled
under the standard returns policy instead.

## What we need

- Your order number
- Photographs of the item showing the problem
- A photograph of the shipping label and outer packaging

Submit these through the Report an issue link on your order page.

## Resolution

Once verified, you choose:
- A replacement, dispatched at no cost, or
- A full refund including any shipping charge paid

You are not asked to pay return shipping for a damaged or incorrect item. In
most cases we do not require the faulty item back at all.

## Manufacturing defects after use

Seams, zips, and fastenings are covered for **90 days** from delivery against
manufacturing defects. Normal wear, shrinkage from incorrect washing, and
damage from alterations are not covered. Contact support with photographs and
we will assess it case by case.
