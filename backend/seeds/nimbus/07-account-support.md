# Account and Support

## Hours

Pharmacist-supported chat and phone are available **Monday to Saturday,
9am to 8pm IST**. Order placement and account access work 24/7; anything
needing pharmacist judgement (a dosage question, an interaction concern, an
order held for review) waits for the next open hour if you contact us outside
these times.

## Contact

- Live chat: available in the app during pharmacist hours.
- Phone: 1800-000-2255, pharmacist hours.
- Email: care@nimbushealth.in, response within 24 hours on business days.

## What we can and cannot advise on

Our support team can help with orders, delivery, prescriptions, refills,
billing, and account questions. **We cannot provide medical advice** —
whether to take a medicine, how much to take, whether to combine two
medicines, or how to manage a health condition. Those questions always go to
a licensed pharmacist or your doctor, never answered directly by chat or by
an automated system, regardless of how the question is phrased.

## Data and privacy

Prescription and health information is sensitive. We store only what is
needed to dispense and deliver your medication safely, and it is never used
for marketing. See our privacy policy for the full detail on retention and
your rights to access or delete your data.
