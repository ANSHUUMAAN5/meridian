# Shipping and Delivery

## Options and cost

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | ₹99, free on orders over ₹1,499 |
| Express | 1-2 business days | ₹199 |

Free standard shipping applies automatically once the cart total, after
discounts, exceeds ₹1,499.

## Dispatch

Orders placed before 2pm IST on a business day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after that, or on a weekend or public holiday, are dispatched
the next business day.

You will receive a tracking link by email and SMS when the parcel leaves our
warehouse.

## Delivery areas

We deliver across India. Some remote PIN codes are served only by Standard
shipping; Express is unavailable there and the option will not appear at
checkout.

## Failed delivery

Couriers attempt delivery up to three times. After the third failed attempt
the parcel returns to us and is refunded automatically, minus the original
shipping charge if one was paid.

## International

We do not currently ship outside India.
