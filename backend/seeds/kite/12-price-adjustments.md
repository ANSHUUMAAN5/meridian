# Price Adjustments and Promotions

## Price drop after purchase

If an item you bought at full price is reduced within **7 days** of your order
date, we refund the difference as store credit. Request it through support
with your order number.

Price adjustments are not available on:
- Items already purchased at a discount
- Orders placed more than 7 days ago
- Clearance and end-of-season sales
- Black Friday and other flash events

## Discount codes

One discount code per order. Codes cannot be stacked, and cannot be combined
with Kite Club point redemption.

If you forgot to apply a code, we cannot add it after the order is placed.
Cancel the order before dispatch and reorder with the code applied.

## Promotional exclusions

Sitewide promotions typically exclude gift cards and collaboration ranges. The
excluded categories are listed in the terms attached to each promotion.

## Price errors

If an item is listed at an obviously incorrect price due to a system error, we
may cancel the order and refund it in full. We will contact you before doing
so.
