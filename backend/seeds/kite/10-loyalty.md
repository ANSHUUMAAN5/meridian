# Kite Club Loyalty Programme

Kite Club is free to join and opens automatically with your first order.

## Earning points

You earn **1 point per ₹100 spent**, calculated on the order value after
discounts and excluding shipping. Points post to your account 30 days after
delivery, once the return window has closed.

Returned items have their points reversed.

## Redeeming points

**500 points can be redeemed for ₹250** off any order. Redemption is in fixed
500-point blocks; partial redemption is not possible.

Points cannot be redeemed on sale items or combined with a promotional
discount code in the same order.

## Tiers

| Tier | Annual spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Member | ₹0 | 1 point per ₹100 |
| Silver | ₹10,000 | 1.25 points per ₹100, free Express shipping |
| Gold | ₹25,000 | 1.5 points per ₹100, free Express, early sale access |

Tier is assessed on a rolling 12-month basis and reviewed monthly.

## Expiry

Points expire **24 months** after they are earned if unused. You are emailed
30 days before any points lapse.
