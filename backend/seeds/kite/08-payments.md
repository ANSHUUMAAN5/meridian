# Payment Methods

## Accepted

- UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and any UPI app)
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
- Net banking from all major Indian banks
- Cash on Delivery, on orders **under ₹5,000**
- Kite & Co gift cards

## Cash on Delivery

COD is available on orders under ₹5,000 to serviceable PIN codes. A ₹49 COD
handling fee applies and is shown at checkout. COD is not available on Express
shipping or on pre-order items.

Repeated refusal of COD deliveries may result in COD being disabled for an
account.

## EMI

No-cost EMI is available on credit cards from participating banks for orders
above ₹3,000, over 3 or 6 months. The option appears at checkout if your card
qualifies.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails but money leaves your account, it is an authorisation hold,
not a charge. Banks release these automatically within 5-7 business days. No
order is created and nothing needs to be done from our side.

## Security

We do not store card numbers. Payments are processed by our PCI-DSS compliant
payment partner.
