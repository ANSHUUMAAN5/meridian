# Refunds

## Timeline

After your return is received and passes inspection, refunds are issued to the
**original payment method within 5-7 business days**. Inspection itself
usually takes 1-2 business days after the parcel arrives at the warehouse.

Bank processing can add a further 2-3 business days before the amount appears
on a statement. We have no control over that portion of the timeline.

## Refund method

Refunds always go back to the payment method used at checkout. We cannot
redirect a refund to a different card, a different UPI ID, or a bank account.

For **Cash on Delivery** orders, we ask for bank details during the return
request, since there is no original payment instrument to credit. COD refunds
take 7-10 business days.

## Partial refunds

If an order qualified for free shipping only because of an item you have since
returned, the shipping charge of ₹99 is deducted from the refund.

Discount codes are not re-issued after a return.

## Refund not received

If 10 business days have passed since we confirmed the refund and nothing has
arrived, contact support with your order number. We will provide an ARN
(Acquirer Reference Number) that your bank can trace.
