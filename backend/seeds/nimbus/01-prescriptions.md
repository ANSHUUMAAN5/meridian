# Prescription Requirements

Nimbus Health dispenses prescription medications only against a **valid
prescription** uploaded or forwarded by your doctor. We cannot dispense a
prescription medication on the strength of a customer's description of what
they usually take.

## Uploading a prescription

Upload a clear photo or PDF of the prescription at checkout, or have your
doctor's clinic send it directly to prescriptions@nimbushealth.in. A
prescription must show the patient's name, the medicine, the dosage, and the
prescribing doctor's registration number.

## Validity

A prescription is valid for **6 months** from the date it was written, unless
the doctor has specified a shorter period on the prescription itself. After
that, a fresh prescription is required — we cannot extend or renew an expired
one on our own judgement.

## Schedule H1 and controlled medicines

Certain medicines (Schedule H1 and narcotic/psychotropic drugs) require the
**original physical prescription**, not a photo, to be retained by our
pharmacist at the time of dispatch, per Indian pharmacy regulation. These
orders cannot be placed as repeat/auto-refill and are reviewed individually by
a licensed pharmacist before dispatch.

## Pharmacist review

Every prescription order is checked by a registered pharmacist before it
ships, regardless of how it was submitted. This can add 2-4 hours to
processing versus an over-the-counter order.
