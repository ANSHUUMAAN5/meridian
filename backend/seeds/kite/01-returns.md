# Returns Policy

Kite & Co accepts returns within **30 days** of the delivery date. The return
window is counted from the day the courier marks the order as delivered, not
the day it was placed.

## Condition requirements

Returned items must be:
- Unworn and unwashed
- Free of stains, odours, pet hair, and alterations
- Accompanied by the original tags, still attached

Items that arrive back at our warehouse failing these conditions are returned
to the customer at their own cost, and no refund is issued.

## What cannot be returned

- **Sale items** are final sale. Anything purchased at a reduced price cannot
  be returned or exchanged, including items bought during seasonal promotions.
- Underwear, swimwear, and pierced jewellery, for hygiene reasons.
- Gift cards.
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

## How to start a return

Open your order in the account area and select **Return items**. Choose a
reason, print the prepaid label, and drop the parcel at any partner pickup
point. Returns are free within India; we do not charge a restocking fee.

Once the warehouse receives and inspects the parcel, the refund is processed.
See the Refunds policy for timelines.
