# Tracking Your Order

## Where to find tracking

A tracking link is emailed and texted when your parcel is dispatched. It is
also on the order page in your account.

Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show movement after dispatch. A link that
shows nothing immediately is normal and not a sign of a problem.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Payment received, not yet picked from the warehouse |
| Processing | Being picked and packed |
| Dispatched | Handed to the courier |
| Out for delivery | On the vehicle, arriving today |
| Delivered | Courier has marked it delivered |

## Marked delivered but not received

This is usually a courier scanning the parcel early, or leaving it with a
neighbour or security desk. Wait 24 hours, then check with anyone who may have
accepted it.

If it has still not appeared after 24 hours, contact support with your order
number. We open an investigation with the courier, which takes up to 5 business
days. If the parcel cannot be located we send a replacement or issue a full
refund.

## Delayed parcels

Contact support if a Standard order has not arrived within 8 business days, or
an Express order within 4 business days.
