# Delivery

## Timelines

| Type | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Over-the-counter | 1-2 business days |
| Prescription (standard review) | 2-3 business days |
| Schedule H1 / controlled | 3-5 business days |

Prescription orders take longer because of mandatory pharmacist review before
dispatch — this cannot be expedited by paying for faster shipping.

## Cold-chain medicines

Certain medicines (insulin, some biologics, some vaccines) ship in
insulated, temperature-controlled packaging and require someone to be present
to receive them. These cannot be left with a neighbour or a security desk.
If nobody is available, the courier will attempt delivery the next business
day; after two failed attempts the order returns to our pharmacy and the
medicine is not resupplied due to cold-chain integrity rules — a fresh order
is required.

## Areas we serve

We currently deliver across India except a small number of remote PIN codes
where cold-chain delivery cannot be guaranteed; the checkout page will tell
you if your address isn't serviceable.

## Address changes

A delivery address can be changed any time before the pharmacist marks the
order as dispatched. Once dispatched, we cannot redirect a prescription
medicine mid-transit for safety and verification reasons — refuse the
delivery instead and contact us to arrange a reship to the correct address.
