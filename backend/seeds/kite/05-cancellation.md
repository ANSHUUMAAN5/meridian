# Order Cancellation

## Before dispatch

Orders can be cancelled free of charge **within 2 hours of being placed**, or
any time before the parcel is dispatched — whichever comes later. Use the
Cancel order button on the order page.

A full refund is issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days.

## After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the
delivery, or accept it and start a return under the standard 30-day policy.

Refusing delivery is treated as a return. The refund is issued once the parcel
comes back to our warehouse, which typically adds 5-7 days to the timeline.

## Partial cancellation

Individual items can be cancelled from a multi-item order as long as that
order has not been dispatched. If cancelling an item drops the order below
₹1,499, the free shipping benefit is withdrawn and ₹99 is deducted from the
refund.

## Cancellations by us

We may cancel an order if an item turns out to be unavailable, if the delivery
address cannot be served, or if a payment fails verification. You are refunded
in full and notified by email.
